HOSPITAL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Emergency Department:
The Emergency Department provides emergency medical assistance 24 hours a day.

Emergency Department Location:
Ground Floor, East Wing.

Emergency Entrance:
The dedicated Emergency Entrance is connected directly to the Emergency Department.

Emergency Services:
The Emergency Department provides immediate medical assessment, first aid, emergency physician consultation, critical care support, and stabilization of emergency patients.

Emergency Contact:
For demonstration purposes, the emergency contact number used by CareCompass is 0866-2222111.

Emergency Route:
From the Main Entrance, proceed to Reception and then follow the signs toward the Emergency Department.

Emergency Availability:
Emergency services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Important:
CareCompass provides hospital navigation and hospital information. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace professional emergency medical care.
