HOSPITAL TREATMENTS

Cardiology:

The cardiology department provides diagnosis and treatment for heart-related conditions. Services include cardiac consultation, ECG, blood pressure monitoring, and heart disease evaluation.

Neurology:

The neurology department provides diagnosis and treatment for neurological conditions. Services include neurological consultation, headache evaluation, seizure evaluation, and nerve-related disorders.

Orthopedics:

The orthopedics department provides diagnosis and treatment for bone, joint, muscle, and ligament-related conditions.

General Medicine:

The general medicine department provides consultation for common illnesses, infections, fever, diabetes management, blood pressure management, and general health problems.
